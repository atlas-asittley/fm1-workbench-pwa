Hold SEQ: the menu for most of this

Hold SEQ about a second. Turn SELECT to pick an action, press SAVE to do it. Clear, paste and repeat ask once more: press SAVE again (HOME cancels).

Write notes one at a time

Stopped: press SEQ, then REC (screen says STEP). Open KEYS and tap notes; each lands on the next step. REC again to finish.

Rest

While writing notes, turn KNOB 2 (STEP) forward to skip steps. One step = a 16th note, so 4 clicks = 1 beat.

Long note

SEQ, turn ALGORITHM to LOCKS. Tap the step after the note and turn KNOB 1 until TIE = ON; do the same for each step it should cover. 2 beats = the note + 7 tied steps. (Not yet confirmed by ear.)

Copy to more measures

Hold SEQ → REPEAT TO 32 (2 bars), 48 or 64 → SAVE, SAVE.
One bar at a time: hold SEQ → COPY PAGE → SAVE, then OCT + → SAVE to paste.

Clear a track

Pick the track with SELECT. Hold SEQ → CLEAR TRACK → SAVE, SAVE. (Also CLEAR STEP, CLEAR PAGE.)

Undo

Hold SEQ → UNDO / REDO → SAVE.

Play from the beginning

PLAY only pauses. Tap ■ STOP (top left): it stops everything and rewinds to step 1. Then PLAY.

Metronome, count-in, loop length

SEQ, turn ALGORITHM to RHYTHM/REC: KNOB 2 click (off / recording / on), KNOB 3 count-in, KNOB 4 length (16 / 32 / 48 / 64 steps).

Blank project

☰ → Files & MIDI → Clear browser saves. Erases all 16 saved projects, so export first.
